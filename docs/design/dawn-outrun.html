DawnOutrun

The neon night drive, recast at sunrise: a haze-blue sky that slowly warms to apricot, with one striped sun on the horizon, around a calm, flat kitchen tool.

  • first light
  • haze
  • airbrushed
  • road-trip
  • unhurried

One move, used three times. The striped sun in its slow sky appears only in the brand mark, the recipe hero and the cooking timer. Lists, plan rows and steps are flat paper and ink.

Light, but still synthwave. The sky runs from haze blue through lavender to apricot, with no hard edges. It reads as 80s and still works in a bright kitchen or a store under fluorescent light.

Built for the stove. The step is 29px bold Barlow in deep indigo (14:1 on white). Oxanium is a quieter retro face, used for headings and the timer only.

Offline uses a different colour. A flat amber banner, set apart from the dusk-blue actions, so a glance tells you nothing has synced yet.

Removed since round 2

  • The perspective grid everywhere: page hero, recipe hero, logo mark and app icon. The sun now sits on a single horizon line.
  • All glows, the chrome wordmark and its sun drop shadow. The wordmark is flat ink Oxanium.
  • VHS scanlines on the offline banner, and the SYNC and YOURS badges. Pending items now get a small amber dot.
  • Sunrise gradients on meal number tiles, progress dots, the week bar and the desktop header and thumbnails. These are now flat ground or dusk blue.
  • Most ink outlines on chips, callouts, the timer and steppers. These are now hairlines or plain fills.

Kept: the striped sun rising out of a slow haze-blue-to-apricot sky.
